# Case Study: Palantir / Acus — Swedish Police (2020–present)
**AI Governance · GRC · Cognitive Security**

> *Part of the [AI Governance Case Studies](../README.md) series — examining real-world AI deployments against EU law, democratic accountability, and cognitive security risks.*

---

> **Status:** In progress. This case study will cover the Swedish Police Authority's use of Palantir's Acus platform — a Swedish adaptation of Palantir Gotham — for predictive analytics and link analysis. Unlike the Clearview case, no IMY investigation has been initiated, no sanction has been issued, and the Police Authority has refused to confirm or deny the relationship, citing national security.
